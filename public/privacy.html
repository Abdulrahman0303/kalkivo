Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Application: Kalkivo
Privacy Summary: Kalkivo respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. All your calculations run 100% locally on your device.

1. Overview

Kalkivo ("we", "our", or "the App") is an everyday utility and calculator application providing free tools for GST calculations, scientific calculations, unit conversions, and finance metrics. This Privacy Policy describes how Kalkivo handles your information.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Kalkivo is designed with privacy at its core:

  • No Personal Data: We do not ask for, collect, store, or process names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • Local Processing: All numbers, formulas, and calculations you enter are processed entirely in memory on your device. No calculation data is transmitted to our servers or any third parties.
  • Local Preferences: User preferences such as theme mode (Dark/Light) are stored solely in your device's browser/local storage for convenience and never leave your device.

3. Permissions

Kalkivo does not request access to sensitive device permissions, including but not limited to:

  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Camera or Microphone
  • Device Storage or Photos

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Kalkivo does not integrate any third-party behavioral tracking SDKs, ad networks, or data brokers. No browsing or usage profiling is conducted.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is suitable for users of all ages, including students. Because Kalkivo does not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly collect or solicit information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this URL with a revised effective date.
